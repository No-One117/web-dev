Halo: Combat Evolved Anniversary Achievements

  • Pillar of Autumn - Complete "The Pillar of Autumn" on any difficulty.
  • Halo - Complete "Halo" on any difficulty.
  • Truth and Reconciliation - Complete "Truth and Reconciliation" on any difficulty.
  • The Silent Cartographer - Complete "The Silent Cartographer" on any difficulty.
  • Assault on the Control Room - Complete "Assault on the Control Room" on any difficulty.
  • 343 Guilty Spark - Complete "343 Guilty Spark" on any difficulty.
  • The Library - Complete "The Library" on any difficulty.
  • Two Betrayals - Complete "Two Betrayals" on any difficulty.
  • Keyes - Complete "Keyes" on any difficulty.
  • The Maw - Complete "The Maw" on any difficulty.
  • Birth of a Spartan - Complete the Halo CE campaign (Legendary).
  • Living Legend - Complete all Halo CE levels on Legendary Solo.
  • Skull Master, Par Time, Par Score achievements - Find all Skulls, complete all levels under Par Time/Score.
