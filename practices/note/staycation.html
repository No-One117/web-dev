Arches National Park Visitor Guide

Your Gateway to Utah's Famous Stone Arches

Visitor Note: This fan-made guide is for informational purposes. Please check the official Arches National Park website for the most current details, conditions, and advisories!

Overview

  • Location: Eastern Utah, outside Moab, USA
  • Size: ~76,679 acres
  • Established: April 12, 1929 (as a National Monument), designated National Park in 1971
  • Highlights: Over 2,000 natural stone arches, spires, and balanced rocks
  • Famous Arches: Delicate Arch, Landscape Arch, Double Arch, and more
  • Best Time to Visit: Spring and fall for mild weather

Notable Arches & Features

  • Delicate Arch - The free-standing arch seen on most Utah license plates
  • Landscape Arch - The longest natural stone arch in the world
  • Double Arch - A pair of archways connected at their bases
  • Balanced Rock - A huge boulder balanced on a narrow rock
  • Fiery Furnace - Maze-like red rock canyons (guided hikes recommended)

Experiences & Activities

  • Hiking: Trails range from short, easy walks (Windows section, Balanced Rock) to longer hikes (Delicate Arch, Devils Garden)
  • Photography: Sunrise and sunset provide great light for dramatic photos
  • Stargazing: Park is renowned for dark skies and brilliant stars
  • Camping: Devils Garden Campground offers sites (book in advance)
  • Guided Tours: Ranger-led programs and Fiery Furnace hikes available
  • Accessibility: Several viewpoints and arches accessible to people with limited mobility

Visitor Tips

  • Park Entrance: Fees apply ($30/private vehicle as of 2025)
  • Reservations: Required for timed entry during busy months (see NPS website)
  • Safety: Bring water, desert conditions! Watch for flash floods and heat
  • Leave No Trace: Please stay on marked trails and respect the fragile landscape
  • Wildlife: Keep a lookout for desert bighorn sheep, lizards, and ravens
